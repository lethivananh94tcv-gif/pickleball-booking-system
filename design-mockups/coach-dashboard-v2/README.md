## Design direction

- Giu identity PickleClub: xanh la, xanh navy va diem nhan vang.
- Dashboard co mat do thong tin cao hon, phu hop tac vu lap lai cua Coach.
- Moi tab co hero tom tat, KPI lien quan, khu thao tac chinh va panel phu.
- Card bo goc nho, it trang tri, uu tien scan nhanh va hanh dong ro rang.
